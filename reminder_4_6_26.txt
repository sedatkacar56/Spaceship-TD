HOW TO ADD TO HOME SCREEN
----------------------------------------------------
iPhone (must use Safari):
  1. Open https://sedatkacar56.github.io/Spaceship-TD/
  2. Tap Share button (box with arrow)
  3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap Add

Android (Chrome):
  - Chrome shows install banner automatically
  - Or: 3-dot menu -> Add to Home Screen

Result: Game opens fullscreen, no browser bar, like a native app.
